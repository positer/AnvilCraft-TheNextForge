# Manual upgrading

1. Right-click the item onto an anvil (or grindstone) to place it.
2. Hold an **anvil hammer**: AnvilCraft anvil hammer, or its royal / frost / ember / transcendence variants.
   A dragon rod or a plain anvil is not a hammer and cannot start a strike.
3. Right-click to strike once; Shift + right-click to take the item back.

Hit chance by level: 90% at 0-3, 76.5% at 4-6, 65% at 7-9, 55.3% at 10, then x0.85 per level, floor 30% from 14.
Each strike costs player experience: `6 + 4 x level`, multiplied by 1.5 every 5 levels after 20.
On a failed roll the outcome depends on material: a plain anvil with a plain hammer can destroy the item; if either side is transcendence it only degrades; an embedded transcendium nugget turns destruction into a level reset.
