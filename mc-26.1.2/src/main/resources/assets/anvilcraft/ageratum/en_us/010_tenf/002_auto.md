# Automatic upgrading

<structure id="../../structures/tnf_auto_hammer.snbt"/>

Bottom to top: the base anvil, the fish tank, and the anvil that falls onto it. Swap the base for a grindstone to
switch to the grinding line.

Place a fish tank directly above an anvil or grindstone, put one forging target inside, and drop anvils onto it.
One landing is one strike.

The tank must be fed AnvilCraft experience fluid; each strike costs the same experience formula as the manual path.
The tank outlet is the master switch: closed means keep upgrading in place, open means eject once the level reaches
the threshold given by the redstone signal (threshold = 2s + 1). Automatic upgrading stops at level 50.
